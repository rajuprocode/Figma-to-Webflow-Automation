The gap

Dehydration doesn’t announce itself. Cognitive performance can decline at around 1% body-mass loss, with strength and endurance following at 2% as heat-related risk rises.

Hydration already matters across performance, safety, and occupational health. What teams often lack is an objective measure they can repeat and act on while it still matters.
